FAQ

Common SiriusXM questions.

Is Sanskar Sharma the official SiriusXM website?

No. We are an independent third-party information resource. Official SiriusXM pages are linked when readers need current account, plan or support information.

Why might a channel be missing from my radio?

Channel availability can depend on the plan and the radio chipset. If a channel is included in the plan but unavailable on the radio, SiriusXM says the content may still be available through its app.

Where can I check current prices?

Use our plans overview as a guide, then verify the current price, offer terms, taxes and fees on SiriusXM's official plans page.
