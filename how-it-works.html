HOW IT WORKS

Learn how SiriusXM listening works.

Depending on your plan and compatible equipment, SiriusXM can be enjoyed in a vehicle and through internet streaming.

01

Vehicle radio

Compatible vehicle radios can receive SiriusXM service. The exact lineup depends on your radio and plan.

02

Mobile app

Supported subscriptions can provide streaming through the SiriusXM app on phones and other compatible devices.

03

Home devices

Compatible smart TVs, speakers and other devices can provide SiriusXM streaming when supported by your plan.

Activation and missing-channel basics

Activation and troubleshooting steps can vary by vehicle. For missing channels, SiriusXM recommends checking plan eligibility, power cycling the radio and, if needed, requesting a signal refresh through official support.
