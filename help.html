Activation

Activation generally requires an eligible subscription and compatible radio/device. Exact steps depend on the vehicle and service. Use official SiriusXM account instructions for the current process.

Missing channels

SiriusXM states that a radio's chipset can affect channel availability. If an included channel is missing, power cycling the vehicle/radio may help; SiriusXM also provides an official signal-refresh option.

Billing & cancellation

We do not process payments or cancel accounts. Use the official SiriusXM account tools or contact SiriusXM directly.
